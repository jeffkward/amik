an agent that needs a decision asks for one, and the card
waits. a question with nothing written under it holds its card: it will
not be worked, and the board says why rather than looking idle.

open it and you will find a questions tab with a box to type in. write
anything — even "either is fine" — and the question is answered.

a card that was waiting on an answer goes back to ready the moment its
last question has one. you do not have to move it yourself; answering
was the whole gesture.

**this card changes nothing in your project.** once it has an answer,
the agent's whole job is to write what you said into the outcome and
close the card. it is the mechanic you are looking at, not the work.

## Questions

### what should this card write in its outcome?

> anything at all. a word, a sentence, "n/a" — the content does not
> matter and nothing is built from it. what matters is that something
> is written here, because an empty section is an unanswered question,
> and an unanswered question holds its card.
