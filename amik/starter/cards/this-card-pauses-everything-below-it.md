the pause toggle is on. a card carrying it lands in review
when its work is done, and nothing ranked below it is worked until you
move it out.

it is how you stop the board on one card without stopping it
altogether. the cards above it still run.

you will see the queue report the pause rather than report an empty
board, which is the difference between "nothing to do" and "waiting on
you".

drag it to ready, or open it with the pencil and change status to
ready.
